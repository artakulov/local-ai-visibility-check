# Check your business in ChatGPT in 20 minutes

No installation. Works in a free account. Same method as the scripts in this repo, done by hand.

## Step 1. Fill in your details

- **Your business:** ______________________
- **Your area** (neighbourhood or city): ______________________
- **Your niche** in English: ______________________
- **Three services you actually sell:** ______________________

Use the services you sell, not your category. "Sub-Zero refrigerator repair" tells you something. "Appliance repair" does not.

## Step 2. Three rules, or the result is meaningless

1. **Ask each question three times, each in a NEW chat.** Answers fluctuate. One answer is not a measurement.
2. **Always the same language.** Switching languages changes the answer more than most other factors.
3. **If the assistant answered without searching the web, mark it "not measured".** That is not a zero. Counting unknowns as zeros makes your situation look worse than it is.

## Step 3. The questions

Replace `{niche}`, `{area}`, `{service}`, `{company}`, `{competitor}`.

**Discovery (these are the ones that count)**
1. `Who is the best {niche} company in {area}? Give specific company names.`
2. `What are the top-rated {niche} providers in {area}?`
3. `Can you recommend a reliable {niche} company in {area}?`
4. `I need {niche} in {area}. Which local businesses should I consider?`
5. `I care about quality and reviews. Which {niche} company in {area} would you recommend?`
6. `Who is the best company for {service 1} in {area}? Give specific company names.`
7. `Who is the best company for {service 2} in {area}? Give specific company names.`
8. `Who is the best company for {service 3} in {area}? Give specific company names.`
9. `I need {service 1} in {area} - which company should I call?`
10. `I need {service 2} in {area} - which company should I call?`

**Brand questions (score these separately, they do NOT count in your main score)**
11. `Is {company} a good choice for {niche} in {area}?`
12. `What do customers say about {company} in {area}?`
13. `Would you recommend {company} for {niche} in {area}?`

**Comparison**
14. `{company} vs {competitor} for {niche} in {area} - which is better?`
15. `How does {company} compare to {competitor} in {area}?`

## Step 4. Score it

For each answer: **1** your website is cited · **0.5** you are named, no link · **0** you are not there · **-** no web search, not measured.

**Your score = (sum of points ÷ number of measured answers) × 100.**

Count only questions 1-10. Score 11-15 separately: being known by name and being found are different problems, and mixing them gives you a flattering number that means nothing.

## Step 6. What to do with it

1. Take your source list from step 5. For each site: do you have a profile, is it claimed, are the address and phone right, are there recent reviews.
2. Yelp, BBB and Angi profiles are free. Bing Places is free. Most people reading this have none of them.
3. Reviews: recent matters more than many. Most consumers want reviews from the last three months.
4. Never pay for reviews or offer anything in exchange. The FTC rule has been in force since October 2024 with penalties up to $53,088 per violation, and Google's 2026 policy suspends profiles for it.
5. Ask whoever builds your website one question: **can AI assistant crawlers see our site, and are our services and prices in the normal page text?** If they do not understand the question, that is your answer.

## What this cannot tell you

Whether it produced a customer. Whether other assistants see you the same way. Whether a change you make caused the score to move. And no one, including any agency, can guarantee that an assistant will mention you.
